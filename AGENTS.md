# Club Leopold | Instructions for AI Agents

This repo is the authoritative project memory for Club Leopold. **Do not begin substantive design, branding, marketing, copy or product work without first reading the approved ethos in [BRAND.md](BRAND.md).**

## Required reading order
1. [BRAND.md](BRAND.md) in full: **A Life With Character**, purpose/mission/vision, customer aspiration, four tensions, brand voice, and how ethos changes products and campaigns.
2. [LEO.md](LEO.md) for **all** depictions or adaptations of Leo. Leo v1 is locked.
3. [PRODUCTS.md](PRODUCTS.md) for apparel, graphics, blanks, and approval status.
4. [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for commercialization, production and social.
5. [assets/README.md](assets/README.md) and relevant approved reference images when visuals are needed.

## Default creative decision test
**Does the product look desirable even before knowing the brand?**
**Does the concept communicate character rather than status?**
**Is it warm, sophisticated, playful in small ways, and free of fake exclusivity?**

Club Leopold is **not** a faux country club, novelty dog merchandise, or an "old money" costume. The philosophy is *good taste and good humor*, expressed through actual garment quality and an interesting, inviting everyday life.

## Safeguards
- Preserve founder-approved Leo anatomy, wardrobe, tote, and embroidery treatment without explicit change approval.
- Respect the approved T-shirt designs and visual references; earlier rejected exploration boards are not canonical.
- Do not confuse approved concepts with real samples, stitch-ready embroidery, cleared IP, licensed fonts, verified unit costs or actual sales.
- Clearly mark unknowns **TBD**, untested ideas **PROPOSED**, and approved founder decisions **LOCKED**.
- Never invent brand heritage, member exclusivity, supplier specs or claims.
- For a newly approved decision, edit the relevant existing Markdown file. Keep the documentation compact.

This file is an agent entry point. **BRAND.md remains the single source of truth for the brand ethos.**
