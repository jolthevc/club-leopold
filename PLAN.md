# Club Leopold | Plan and Next Steps

**Current phase:** early product validation. Working experimental budget ~**$200**. No live sales, published storefront, confirmed supplier economics or trademark clearance.

## Immediate plan
1. Preliminary US trademark, domain and handle review for **Club Leopold**, with proper professional clearance before significant brand spend.
2. Keep current approved Leo and T-shirt artwork in assets/ and treat all older explorations as superseded.
3. Find POD provider that supports exact blank and decoration configuration. Compare **Apliiq, Printful and Printify**; don't assume every provider allows combined embroidery and back print for AS Colour 5026.
4. Commission/vendor-digitize the 3-color head emblem and order a **real 2–2.5 inch embroidered sample** on ecru fabric. Assess legibility, wear comfort, thread coverage.
5. Quote and sample Core Tee and Story Tee; compare physical print and fabric quality. Inspect wash results and on-body fit.
6. Design the crewneck and hoodie after T-shirt quality is established.
7. Launch lean storefront with true photos, sizing, delivery/return terms and realistic pricing.
8. Build curated Instagram profile as digital showroom, then test a small amount of paid traffic after product approval.

## Pricing and economics
- Working **not validated** retail: Core Tee $45, crewneck $85, hoodie $95; Story Tee TBD.
- Example earlier $200 allocation: ~$50 flagship sample, ~$22 secondary sample, ~$105 ads, ~$23 contingency. Reallocate to actual stitchouts and two-sided tee sample if needed; sample costs may exceed these allowances.
- Count blank, embroidery digitization, per-placement decoration, shipping, processing, taxes where relevant, returns, and customer acquisition before declaring contribution margin.
- POD facilitates learning with low inventory risk; it is not guaranteed to deliver custom luxury manufacturing quality.
- A $200 ad test is directional, never statistically conclusive.

## Social and acquisition
- Create Instagram even with few followers, but first publish roughly nine **beautiful, coherent** posts, mixing Leo, approved art, product details and **real sample photographs** when available.
- Treat the page as a credibility-building boutique showroom, not a content treadmill.
- Do not buy followers or pretend the company has a history it doesn't have.
- Run purchase-oriented ads toward a good product page, measure real traffic, add-to-cart and paid orders; be skeptical of likes alone.
- Later: selective gifting to fashion creators with proper disclosure; customer referrals and organic sharing.
