# Leo | Canonical Character v1

**STATUS: LOCKED.** This file takes precedence over older exploratory boards and incidental mistakes in AI renders.

## Anatomy and face
- Original personified **basset hound**, upright, dressed and human-like in posture, but never a generic human body with a dog head.
- **Very long, heavy, smooth, low-set ears below the jaw**, not silky spaniel feathering; broad muzzle, loose jowls, heavy wrinkled brow.
- Half-lidded eyes, one brow subtly raised, knowing gaze and small closed-mouth smirk.
- Big head, elongated torso, short legs; recognizable basset silhouette, with ear/jaw/shoulder negative space.
- Expressions may vary: amused, skeptical, quietly pleased, caught in the act. No constant sleepy stare, oversized grin or tongue-out version.

## Locked default outfit
- **Navy blazer** with small **gold-toned serif CL monogram** on the chest pocket. No other pocket crest.
- Green-and-cream horizontal rugby stripe shirt underneath.
- Cream cuffed trousers.
- **Brown penny loafers** and visible **burgundy-and-navy striped socks**.
- **No hat. No glasses. No red shoes.**

## Prop
The one signature prop is a **cream canvas tote with green trim**, identical across scenes where used. No extra accessories unless explicitly approved for a new scene.

## Art direction
Flat premium heritage printed-ink illustration. Strong clean contours, matte fills, minimal shading, no gradients or film-animation 3D modeling. Approved palette: forest, burgundy, navy, cream, camel, rust. Graphic discipline of mid-century flat forms and clean European outlines, with dry Ivy-cartoon expression. Keep character anatomy constant even in dynamic poses.

## Signature Leo head emblem | creatively approved
Plain head only, **no wreath/crest**. Exactly **three flat embroidery colors**:
1. **Navy:** strong consistent outline, eyes, nose and facial features.
2. **Rust:** single simple solid shape per ear and head patches.
3. **Cream:** broad muzzle and central blaze, enclosed by navy so it reads on ecru.

One half-lidded eye curve with pupil, one eyebrow line, no whisker dots, fur shading, highlights, gradients or tiny internal texture. Aim for stitchable roughly 1 mm contour and no elements smaller than ~2 mm at final size, but the digitizer must verify actual tolerances. Size comparisons: **2.5, 2.0 and 1.5 inches wide**. Two-inch sample is the key test; none of the AI-rendered samples proves real stitching.

**Tiers:** full-color illustration for scenes; simplified 3-color embroidery; micro head emblem for labels (may need further reduction at tiny scale).
