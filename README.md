# Club Leopold

**Premium heritage-inspired clothing & leisure.** Club Leopold is an emerging clothing and lifestyle brand featuring **Leo**, an original, personified basset hound with understated humor and excellent taste.

This is the **small, durable source of truth** for the brand. A future AI collaborator should be able to read these five documents and understand what is approved, what has changed, and what's next.

## Read in order
1. [BRAND.md](BRAND.md): Brand personality, audience, colors, typography and visual system.
2. [LEO.md](LEO.md): **Locked Leo v1** anatomy, expression, wardrobe, tote and head emblem.
3. [PRODUCTS.md](PRODUCTS.md): The **approved Core Tee and Story Tee**, plus planned crewneck and hoodie.
4. [PLAN.md](PLAN.md): Launch, POD, social, economics, next decisions.
5. [assets/README.md](assets/README.md): Visual-asset manifest, approval status and provenance.

## Rules for AI collaborators
- Read these files before designing; use the reference images in assets/ where available.
- **Never change Leo v1 or the approved tees silently.** Older hat, glasses, argyle socks, red shoe and alternate blazer crest designs were exploratory and superseded.
- **One side stays quiet and the other tells a story.** Fashion desirability comes before mascot novelty.
- Distinguish **LOCKED** creative decisions, **PROPOSED** product plans and **TBD** unverified details.
- AI renders and embroidery simulations are not production art, stitchouts, real garments or verified product claims.
- Do not invent trademark rights, exact font licensing, manufacturing specs, costs, availability or results.
- Keep this repository lean; update the existing topical Markdown files rather than proliferating new documentation.
