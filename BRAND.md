# Club Leopold | Brand Foundation

**Status:** Creative direction approved. Legal and manufacturing work remains pending.

## Identity
Club Leopold is an elegant, warm, slightly eccentric premium clothing and lifestyle brand, starting with T-shirts, a crewneck and a hoodie. Original mascot **Leo** is a personified basset hound. Product attractiveness comes before mascot novelty. Long-term ambition extends to rugby shirts, knitwear, outerwear, accessories and other lifestyle categories.

**Design rule: One side stays quiet and the other tells a story.** The garment must be wearable and desirable even before customers know Leo.

## Positioning and audience
Timeless American/British leisurewear with a contemporary fit and a distinctive editorial-illustration sensibility. Understated sophistication and quiet wit, never childlike cartoon merchandise, dog puns, fake aristocracy, giant logos or invented club history.

Initial customer hypothesis: style-aware adults around 22–34, menswear-led but unisex, who appreciate heritage clothing, elevated basics and boutique illustration. This is not validated customer data.

Creative reference territory (inspiration only, do not copy): Polo Ralph Lauren, Aimé Leon Dore, Maison Kitsuné, Drake's, Japanese vintage illustration.

## Visual and typography
- Main wordmark: elegant, heritage-inspired serif **Club Leopold**, restrained weight and carefully spaced.
- Core Tee rear neck: small navy serif **Club Leopold**.
- Story Tee large back: smaller regular/thin serif **CLUB LEOPOLD**, wide tracking, gently arched, ample space above the illustration. Avoid bold, crowded type.
- Typeface name, custom vector master, font license and exact tracking: **TBD**. The approved design is visual, not an identified licensed font file.
- Art: clean flat printed ink, confident outlines, minimal shading, coherent restricted color.
- Photography: warm daylight, ecru textiles, natural people, tactile real garments, understated editorial lifestyle.
- Voice: warm, precise, lightly witty; no forced luxury, faked exclusivity, untrue brand story or inflated quality claims.

## Brand system
1. The Club Leopold wordmark, usable without Leo.
2. Leo's plain three-color head emblem.
3. Full upright Leo in scenes and outfits, governed by LEO.md.
4. Future garment graphics without Leo where useful.

**Legal note:** Club Leopold is the creatively selected name, **not trademark-cleared**. US clothing/retail and adjacent-category checks are still required.
