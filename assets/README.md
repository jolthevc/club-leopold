## Authority
- **LEO.md is the definitive rulebook** for the animal's face, outfit and emblem. Any accidental differences in a concept board are not newly approved changes.
- **PRODUCTS.md is definitive** for placement, design status and current unverified garment/supplier hypotheses.
- The images above are original exported render boards from the approved design sessions, saved at their original provided resolution.
- Do not confuse photographs of **simulated** stitching or AI-generated lifestyle models with real manufactured merchandise. No actual stitchout, physical garment sample or licensed vector logo has been approved.
