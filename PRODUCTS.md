## Production checklist for every item
Confirm garment and decoration provider; obtain quote for blank, embroidery, any second print location, shipping, fees and rework; convert approved artwork to editable master; digitize embroidery; stitch real sample; test print handfeel/color/garment wash/fit; photograph actual product; only then approve for store listing.

**Never equate an AI mockup, embroidery simulation or 'actual-size' illustration on a board with an inspected physical production sample.**
